Roleplay Simulator

Practice cold calling against an AI customer. Handle their objections and book a meeting.

How it works: The AI plays a resistant customer trying to end the call. You have 6-8 exchanges. Your responses are scored on: rapport building, objection handling, discovery questions, and closing technique. At the end you get a scorecard with coaching tips.

💡 Pro tip: Use the customer's name, acknowledge their situation before pitching, and use permission-based language ("would it be worth…") for best results.
